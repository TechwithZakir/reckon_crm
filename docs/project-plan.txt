```text
/crm
```

# 2. Mandatory Architecture Rules

Codex must strictly follow these rules.

```text
1. Frappe CRM is the core CRM foundation.

2. Use native Frappe CRM records:
   - CRM Lead
   - CRM Deal
   - CRM Organization
   - Contact
   - CRM Task
   - CRM Call Log
   - Communication
   - Notes
   - Activities

3. Do NOT use ERPNext Lead, Opportunity, Customer, Item,
   or Quotation as the primary CRM entities.

4. ERPNext must remain optional.

5. HRMS must remain optional.

6. WhatsApp must reuse existing Frappe CRM / frappe_whatsapp integration.

7. Meta Lead sync must be reused if already installed.

8. Do NOT fork frappe/crm.

9. Do NOT directly modify apps/crm.

10. All Reckon-specific business logic belongs inside reckon_crm.

11. Any frontend coupling to Frappe CRM must be isolated inside
    a small compatibility/adapter layer.

12. The app must work even when ERPNext is not installed.

13. The app must work even when HRMS is not installed.

14. Mobile/PWA support is mandatory.

15. Security, permission checks, and auditability must be built in
    from the beginning.
```
